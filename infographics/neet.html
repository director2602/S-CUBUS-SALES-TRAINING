02 · Eligibility

1
Age: 17 years by 31 December of the admission year. No upper age limit.
2
Class 12: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology and English. Class 12 students can apply in their board year.
3
PCB marks: 50% General · 45% General-PwD · 40% SC/ST/OBC.
4
Attempts: no limit — droppers are common, so plan a realistic repeat strategy.
5
One score, many courses: MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, BVSc (AIQ) and MBBS abroad all need a NEET qualification.

03 · Difficulty by subject

PhysicsToughest · numerical, time-hungry
ChemistryModerate · NCERT-heavy inorganic
BiologyMost scoring · tricky statements
Overall: moderate in concepts, high in competition. 180 questions in 195 minutes ≈ 1 minute per question, so speed and accuracy decide ranks. Negative marking punishes guessing.

04 · Relation with CBSE & NCERT

Syllabus: notified by NMC; built on NCERT Class 11 + 12 Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
Biology: questions track NCERT lines, diagrams and examples closely — read NCERT line by line, more than once.
Chemistry: inorganic and much of organic come straight from NCERT text and tables.
Physics: NCERT concepts, but marks come from numerical practice beyond the textbook.
Class 11 matters: Class 11 and 12 both carry big weight — a weak Class 11 is the most common reason for a low score.
Boards: CBSE boards and NEET overlap heavily; boards need written answers, NEET needs speed — prepare both together.

Biology
NCERT — the single most important book
Objective NCERT at your Fingertips — MTG
Trueman's Elementary Biology (Vol 1 & 2)
NCERT exemplar + previous papers
Rule for counsellors: NCERT first, one reference book per subject, then daily practice papers and mocks. More books ≠ more marks.

08 · Not selected? Strong options

Repeat with a plan
A focused repeater year: diagnose topic gaps from the last attempt, fix routine, take full mocks every week.
Allied health
B.Sc Nursing, BPT (Physiotherapy), B.Pharm / Pharm.D, BMLT, radiology, optometry, OT technology.
Science via CUET-UG
B.Sc Biotechnology, Microbiology, Zoology, Biochemistry, Forensic Science, Psychology at central universities.
Research track
IISER Aptitude Test (BS-MS) · NEST for NISER & UM-DAE CEBS · B.Sc (Research) programmes.
Agriculture & veterinary
B.Sc Agriculture via ICAR (CUET-UG) · BVSc state-quota seats · Dairy, fisheries, food technology.
MBBS abroad
Only NMC-compliant universities; plan for FMGE/NExT licensing; check total cost and language.
