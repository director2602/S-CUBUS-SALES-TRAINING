Olympiads for Class 8, 9 & 10

Which exam, when, what pattern, how many go ahead at each stage, and which books
समर्पण · सुनिश्चित · सफलता
Why olympiads?
They train the thinking JEE and NEET reward: concepts over rote, multi-step problems, speed with accuracy.
Two kinds
HBCSE track (NSEJS, IOQM) leads to the international olympiads. School olympiads (SOF, NSTSE, VVM) build confidence and practice.
Honest advice
A camp-level result is rare. Use olympiads to deepen learning, never as a promise of selection.

Stage 2 needs at least the minimum admissible score (50% of the average of the top 10) plus state-wise proportional seats. A student who writes NSEJS cannot write a senior NSE (NSEP/NSEC/NSEB/NSEA) the same year.

04 · Difficulty & relation with CBSE / NCERT

SOF · NSTSE · VVM
Easy–moderate. NCERT and CBSE Class-level syllabus with application questions. Good first olympiad.
NSEJS
Hard. NCERT Class 8–10 is only the start; questions use Class 11 ideas and data reasoning.
IOQM
Very hard. Number theory, combinatorics, geometry and algebra well beyond the CBSE syllabus.

06 · What to suggest, class by class

Class 8
Start with SOF ISO/IMO and NSTSE for confidence · VVM for curiosity · try IOQM for exposure · build NCERT Class 8 strongly.
Class 9
NSEJS for strong science students · IOQM for maths lovers · keep one school olympiad · begin IIT/NEET foundation habits.
Class 10
NSEJS and IOQM as final junior attempts · protect board marks · choose the JEE or NEET path for Class 11 using Aptitude + Career Discovery.
S-CUBUS School + Olympiads
For Classes 8–10: strong school concepts, dedicated Olympiad tests, checked practice notebooks and doubt support till 9 PM — with a workload the student can sustain.
