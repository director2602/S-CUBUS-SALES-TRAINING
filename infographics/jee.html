JEE Main: moderate to tough. Speed and accuracy over 180 minutes; 1 percentile can mean thousands of ranks.
JEE Advanced: among the toughest exams anywhere. Multi-concept questions, partial marking, pattern changes yearly.

04 · Relation with CBSE & NCERT

Syllabus: JEE Main follows the NTA syllabus, built on Class 11 + 12 Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
Chemistry: inorganic and much of organic come directly from NCERT text — read it line by line.
Physics & Maths: NCERT gives the concepts; JEE needs far deeper problem practice.
JEE Advanced: similar topics, but depth and question style go well beyond NCERT and CBSE.
Boards still count: 75% in Class 12 is needed for NIT/IIIT/GFTI admission — never ignore the board exam.
Class 11 matters: mechanics, calculus basics and physical chemistry from Class 11 underpin most of the paper.

09 · Not selected? Strong options

State & Delhi counselling
JAC Delhi (DTU, NSUT, IIIT-D, IGDTUW) · state CETs like MHT-CET, WBJEE, KCET, AP/TS EAPCET, COMEDK.
Private university exams
BITSAT, VITEEE, SRMJEEE, MET (Manipal) — separate exams, separate dates; register early.
Research & maths
IISER Aptitude Test · NEST (NISER, CEBS) · ISI (B.Stat, B.Math) · CMI entrance.
Science & computing via CUET-UG
B.Sc Maths, Physics, Computer Science, Data Science, BCA at central and Delhi University colleges.
Defence & maritime
NDA (UPSC) · Army 10+2 Technical Entry · Merchant Navy through IMU-CET.
