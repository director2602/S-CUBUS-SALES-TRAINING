01 · NMMS — National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (Delhi)

Class 8 · conducted by SCERT Delhi
Who can apply
Class 8 students in Delhi government, government-aided or local-body schools · 55% in Class 7 (50% SC/ST) · family income up to ₹3.5 lakh a year. Private-school students are not eligible.
Exam pattern
MAT (Mental Ability) 90 MCQs + SAT (Scholastic Aptitude) 90 MCQs: Science 35, Social Science 35, Maths 20 · 90 minutes each · pass 40% in each (32% SC/ST).
To keep it
₹1,000 a month, Class 9–12 · promotion each year · 55% in Class 9 and 11 · 60% in Class 10 boards · renew on the National Scholarship Portal.
Timeline: forms open around Sep–Oct (2026: 11 Sep – 27 Oct, online) · exam in winter · syllabus = NCERT Class 7–8 + reasoning.

02 · Schools of Specialised Excellence (SoSE)

Free Delhi government schools with focused streams. Entry through an aptitude test.
STEM · Class 9 & 11
Humanities · Class 9 & 11
Performing & Visual Arts · Class 9
21st-Century Skills · Class 9
Armed Forces Preparatory · written test + fitness + interview + medical
Eligibility: Delhi residents · Class 9 entry age 13–15 · Class 11 age 15–17.
STEM test: mental ability, maths and science from the previous class · no negative marking.
When: forms on edudel.nic.in around December.

03 · CM SHRI schools

Delhi's newer model government schools (75 launched in February 2026). Admission by entrance test.
Class 6
300 marks · bilingual

400 marks · English
Pattern: objective OMR test · negative marking for Class 9 and 11.
Eligibility: Delhi residents studying in a recognised Delhi school · half the seats for Delhi government-school students.
When (2026): registration March–May · Class 9 results end of April.

05 · Who should try what

Government-school student, Class 8
NMMS first (₹48,000 over four years) · SoSE or CM SHRI Class 9 test · one school olympiad.
Private-school student, Class 8–9
Not eligible for NMMS · CM SHRI Class 9 test is open to Delhi residents · NSEJS / IOQM + SOF for a strong foundation.
Class 10 student
Board marks first · SoSE / CM SHRI Class 11 STEM test · plan the JEE or NEET path; DTU, NSUT and IIIT-Delhi later admit through JEE Main.
Delhi books & prep
NMMS: NCERT Class 7–8 (Science, Social Science, Maths) + R.S. Aggarwal reasoning + NMMS previous papers. SoSE / CM SHRI: NCERT of the previous class + mental-ability practice + published sample papers on edudel.nic.in.
